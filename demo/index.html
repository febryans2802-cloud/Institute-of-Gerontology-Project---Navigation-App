Cane Guidance Simulator

A walk-through of the two guidance conditions in the study: Screen (look at the phone) and Audio + Haptic (phone stays in the pouch, guidance by vibration and voice). The researcher console on the left is what the walking researcher presses in the Wizard-of-Oz setup.

This is a demo of the interface only. The walker moves the same way in both conditions; the simulator does not predict which condition is better. Phone vibration needs Android Chrome; elsewhere it is shown on screen and can be played as sound.
